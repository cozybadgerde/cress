# Third-party notices

See [`go.mod`](../go.mod) for Go dependencies and
[`go.sum`](../go.sum) for their checksums.

Releases at <https://github.com/cozybadgerde/cress/releases> include
dependency-license reports and archive notices. The
[release guide](RELEASE.md#generated-reports) explains their format and
generation. Dependency records are generated during packaging rather than
maintained as a second catalog here.
