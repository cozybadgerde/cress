# Privacy

This page is a placeholder too. Replace it with a notice that matches what your
site really does, or delete the file and its `[nav.footer]` entry.

A site built with Cress is plain static HTML: no cookies, no tracking, no
forms, and no database. What actually gets collected comes down to where you
host it, since most hosts log IP addresses and user agents, and to anything you
embed yourself, like fonts, analytics, or an embedded video.

Worth covering here:

- who runs the site, and how a visitor can get in touch
- what your host logs, and how long it keeps those logs
- anything the page loads from another domain
- how someone asks about the data you hold on them

Cress does not give legal advice. Check what your jurisdiction actually asks
for, and write this page accordingly.
